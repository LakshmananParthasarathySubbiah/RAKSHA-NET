Fall detection

How a prediction becomes an emergency SMS: the laptop confirms the fall, the board sounds the buzzer and waits for the cancel button, then the SIM800L sends the alert.

  1. Normal monitoringModel predicts straight.
  2. Suspected fallFall with P ≥ 0.70, fewer than 3 samples in a row.
  3. Cancel windowPRED,FALL sent. Buzzer on, 12 s to press the button.
  4. Confirmed fallNo cancel within 12 s.
  5. Emergency SMSSIM800L sends to 2 recipients.

Room localization

The ESP32 scans for BLE beacons named ROOM_Bedroom, ROOM_Hallroom and ROOM_Washroom every 3.5 s and reports the strongest one above −95 dBm.

No BLE room data in this sourceRoom data comes from the firmware's sensor text lines in live mode, or from a replayed live recording.

Position is room-level only: the firmware reports the nearest beacon and its RSSI, not the RSSI of the other beacons, so the wearer's position inside a room is not known. Rooms turn red while a fall alert is open.
